# Credit Scoring Ethics Analysis

## Project Type
Academic Project | Baylor University

## Project Overview
Analyzed ethical concerns in credit scoring systems, focusing on fairness, transparency, privacy, bias, and regulatory compliance in financial decision-making.

## Tools & Skills Used
- Data Ethics
- Business Analysis
- Financial Analytics
- Regulatory Compliance
- Ethical Frameworks
- Risk Assessment
- Critical Analysis

## Key Work Performed
- Evaluated ethical implications of credit scoring systems
- Examined fairness and transparency concerns
- Analyzed regulatory frameworks such as FCRA, Basel II, and IFRS 9
- Applied ethical theories including deontology, utilitarianism, and social contract theory
- Assessed risks of algorithmic bias in financial systems

## My Contribution
Conducted ethics analysis, applied ethical frameworks, interpreted regulatory considerations, and developed recommendations for responsible data practices.
